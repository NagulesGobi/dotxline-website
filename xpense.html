Built natively for iPhone, with the calm design of iOS 26. Numbers first, nothing in your way.

Log

Faster than forgetting.

  • Expenses and income in seconds, with categories and optional receipt photos.
  • Quick Add tiles in your own order. One tap opens a new expense.
  • Widgets and Siri. Quick Add and Today on your Home Screen, and an “Add an expense” shortcut. Nothing saves until you confirm.
  • Reminders if you want them. Local notifications only, off until you switch them on.

Understand

See where it went.

  • History with search, period and category filters, grouped by day.

  • Spent less? Xpense notices and says so. Spent more? It stays neutral.

Plan and share

Stay on budget, together.

  • Budgets for the month and for each category.
  • Recurring bills and savings goals, with the monthly amount you need to hit a deadline.
  • Split with friends in shared groups for flats and trips, then settle up.
  • Works offline and syncs when you’re back. Export to CSV any time.

Small spends add up.

Money rarely goes in one big purchase. It slips away a few pounds at a time, in coffees, trains and snacks you stop noticing. Xpense makes logging them quicker than forgetting them, so your month adds up to a number you can trust, and the part you kept is there to see.
