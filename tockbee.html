What it does

Your hours, your pay, your record

01

Clocks you in for you

When you arrive at work and out when you leave, by location or by your work Wi-Fi, with a notification and Undo every time.

02

Works out your pay

Overtime, weekend and night rates, and unpaid breaks, all counted.

03

Estimates your take-home

After UK income tax (including Scottish rates), National Insurance, student loan and pension.

04

Checks your payslip

Compares your payslip with your shifts and shows any hours that are missing.

05

Timesheet to share

A PDF or CSV with every shift and how it was recorded, ready to send.

06

Stays on your phone

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your record is yours.
