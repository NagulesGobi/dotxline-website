The ring

Arrive. You’re clocked in.

Tockbee clocks you in when you reach work, by location or by your work Wi-Fi. Every automatic clock-in comes with a notification and Undo.

The hand

Every minute counts.

Overtime, weekend and night rates and unpaid breaks, worked out as you go. Then an estimated take-home after tax, National Insurance, student loan and pension.

The tip

Check it against your payslip.

The yellow tip is the moment you clock in. Type in your payslip and Tockbee shows any hours or pay that went missing.

Every hour you work should show up on your payslip. Tockbee keeps your own honest record, on your phone and nobody else’s, so checking takes a minute.

Today

Clocked in, automatically.

The running shift sits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The Clock button always works too, for the days you’d rather tap.

Shifts

Every shift, and how it was recorded.

Times, breaks and whether each shift came from location, Wi-Fi or a tap. Your record explains itself.

Pay

Gross, overtime and take-home.

This week and this pay period, with your goal, before tax and after. Tax figures are estimates; your payslip is the official figure.

Payslip check

Possibly underpaid? You’ll know.

Copy in what your payslip says. Tockbee compares it with the shifts you logged for the same dates and shows the difference.

Export

Your timesheet, ready to share.

A PDF or CSV with every shift and how it was recorded. Choose what goes in, then share it with your manager, keep it, or print it.

Everything it does.

Made for UK hourly, shift and gig workers. Plain words: rota, payslip, take-home.

Clock

It knows when you’re at work.

  • Automatic clock-inBy location at a work site, or by joining your work Wi-Fi.
  • Undo, every timeEach automatic clock-in sends a notification you can reverse.
  • Live Activity and widgetsFor the Lock Screen, the Dynamic Island and the Home Screen.
  • Rota remindersSo a shift never starts unrecorded.

Pay

What it should pay.

  • Rates and rulesOvertime, weekends, nights and unpaid breaks.
  • Estimated take-homeAfter PAYE (including Scottish rates), National Insurance, student loan and pension.
  • Holiday accrualAt 12.07%, plus tips and business miles at HMRC rates.
  • Universal Credit periodsIf your pay is assessed that way.

Record

Your record, your call.

  • Payslip checkShows any hours or pay that went missing.
  • Timesheet packA PDF with every shift, how it was recorded and a record check, with a letter template and Acas guidance.
  • CSV exportFor spreadsheets.
  • Face ID lockKeeps it private on a shared phone.

Your shift, itemised.

Every shift, how it was recorded and what it should pay, laid out like a receipt you can check against your payslip.

Data not collected.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Tockbee doesn’t have a server to send your hours to.
