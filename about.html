Three habits, in every app.

The same rules for Xpense, for Tockbee, and for whatever we make next.

01

Clear.

  • We say the number. “£3.40 logged to Food.” Plain, specific, no guessing.
  • Honest about estimates. Tockbee calls tax “estimated take-home”. Your payslip is the official figure.

02

Calm.

  • Quiet interfaces that stay out of your way and respect your attention.
  • No guilt, no alarms. Xpense celebrates spending less and stays neutral about spending more. Missing pay is a calm fact, not a siren.

03

Private.

  • No ads. No selling your data. No tracking you across other companies’ apps.
  • As little as possible. Xpense keeps receipt photos on your iPhone. Tockbee collects no data at all.
