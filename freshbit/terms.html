1. What FreshBit is

FreshBit is a free news app for designers and developers. It finds news published by companies and news outlets, groups reports about the same event, and shows a short description written by FreshBit with a link to the original article.

FreshBit is free. There are no payments, subscriptions or adverts. To use FreshBit you sign in with your Apple account, which keeps your saved stories and settings in step across your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

2. Our summaries and the original news

Headlines, summaries and briefs in FreshBit are written in our own words, automatically, by our software (which may include AI). They describe the facts that publishers have reported. They are not the publisher's headline or text, and they are not endorsed by the publisher.

The original articles, headlines, photos and trade marks belong to their publishers and owners. FreshBit does not copy articles. Every story links to its source so you can read it in full there.

We only show images that a publisher offers in its own news feed for syndication, or images released under an open licence (such as Creative Commons), always with a credit. Where neither is available we show our own artwork, which may be an illustration made with AI and is labelled as such.

3. Credibility labels

"Confirmed", "Reported" and "Developing" are produced automatically from rules about who published a story and how many independent sources report it. They are a helpful guide, not a guarantee that a story is true or complete.

Automatic summaries can contain mistakes. Always check the original source before relying on any story. Nothing in FreshBit is professional, legal, financial or investment advice.
