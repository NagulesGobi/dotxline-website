Support/Help with FreshBit

How can we help?

Answers to common questions. If you’re stuck or found a story that looks wrong, email hello@dotxline.com and we’ll get back to you.

Email supportReport a wrong story

Using the feed

  • Pull down on Today to check for new stories.
  • Swipe a story right to save it, left to hide it.
  • Bring hidden stories back in Settings › Hidden stories.
  • Tap the leaf on Today to open your brief.

Choosing what you see

  • Turn topics on or off in Settings › Topics.
  • Mute any of the 51 sources in Settings › Sources.
  • To see only Confirmed stories, turn off Show Reported and Developing stories.

Alerts

  • Turn on Top story alerts in Settings. iOS asks for permission once.
  • Add words in Keyword alerts. Keyword alerts need Top story alerts on.
  • Pick a time for the Morning brief.
  • Not getting alerts? Check iOS Settings › Notifications › FreshBit.

Installing on Mac

  • Download FreshBit for Mac.
  • Open the download and drag FreshBit to Applications.
  • It needs macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
  • FreshBit for Mac also lives in the menu bar for a quick look at the top stories.

Account

  • FreshBit asks you to Sign in with Apple the first time. There’s no password, and Apple lets you hide your email.
  • Your account keeps saved stories and settings in step on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, and brings them back after a reinstall.
  • Delete your account in Settings › Account › Delete account. Its data is deleted straight away.

Common questions

Why does a story say Developing?

Only one outlet has reported it so far. FreshBit checks again every 30 minutes, and the label updates when more trusted sources report it. Tap Follow on the story to get one alert when it becomes Confirmed.

Why is a story missing?

It may have scored under 40, be from a community site only, be off-topic (deals, stock prices or gossip), or be from a source or topic you switched off. You may also have hidden it: check Settings › Hidden stories.

A story looks wrong. What do I do?

Email hello@dotxline.com with the headline and what looks wrong. Always check the original with “Read on …”.

Why is the headline different from the publisher’s?

FreshBit writes every headline, summary and brief in its own words, from the facts publishers reported. It never shows a publisher’s headline or article text. Tap “Read on …” to read the original. See Copyright & Sources.

How do I join the test?

Email hello@dotxline.com for a TestFlight invite. You’ll need Apple’s free TestFlight app on your iPhone.
