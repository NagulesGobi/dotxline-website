Privacy Policy

This policy explains what FreshBit does with personal data, under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. The data controller is dotXline LTD, a company registered in England and Wales. Last updated 12 November 2026.

The short version

FreshBit has no adverts, no tracking and no analytics, and we never sell data. You sign in with Apple, and Apple lets you hide your email address from us.

What we hold, and why

Account (Sign in with Apple): an Apple account ID, plus your name and email only if you choose to share them (Apple lets you hide your email). Used to give you your account and sync. Lawful basis: performing our agreement with you.

Synced data: your saved stories, hidden stories, read history, followed stories, keyword alerts and app settings. Used to restore them after a reinstall and on your other devices. Lawful basis: performing our agreement with you.

Alerts (only if you turn them on): a push notification token, the topics, keywords, brief time and time zone you choose, and which alerts were sent, so we do not repeat them. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw by turning alerts off.

Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that run FreshBit for us, under contracts that protect your data: Supabase (database and servers, hosted in London, UK), Apple (Sign in with Apple and notifications) and Expo (delivering notifications).

News text is processed to write our summaries; this never includes your personal data.

Some providers may process data outside the UK (for example in the US). Where they do, transfers are protected by UK-approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Account and synced data: until you delete your account in Settings, which deletes it straight away. Alert data: until you turn alerts off or the token stops working. Server logs: a short period set by our hosting provider.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your account yourself in Settings, or email hello@dotxline.com. We reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). We would appreciate the chance to help first.

Children

FreshBit is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app.
