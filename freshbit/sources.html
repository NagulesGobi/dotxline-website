Copyright & Sources

How FreshBit treats other people's work. We built FreshBit to point readers to original journalism and company announcements, not to replace them.

Last updated
12 November 2026
Applies to

Settings › Copyright & Sources

Our own words

Every headline, summary and brief you see is newly written by FreshBit in its own words. Facts and news are not owned by anyone, but the way a publisher writes about them is, so we never show a publisher's headline or article text. In the UK, even headlines can be protected by copyright.

Images

Photos are not covered by the UK copyright exception for reporting current events, so we are careful with them. We only show an image if the publisher offers it in its own news feed for syndication, or if it is released under an open licence such as Creative Commons. Each image shows its credit and, where there is one, a link to its licence.

If no allowed image exists, FreshBit shows its own artwork instead: an illustration it creates with AI from its own headline (never from the publisher's photo), without logos, text or real people, and labelled as an AI illustration so it is never mistaken for a photo of the event.

Credit and links

Every story names its sources and links to the original article. Opening "Read on…" takes you to the publisher's own site, where their terms apply.

Where the news comes from

Public news feeds (RSS) published by companies and outlets for exactly this kind of use. You can see every source, and switch any of them off, in Settings, Sources.

Request a change or removal

If you own content and want it changed or removed, email hello@dotxline.com with a link to the story and what you own. We act on genuine requests quickly.
